COMMON QUESTIONS

Clarity first.

Is FigSuite a trading platform?

No. The beta is a simulation and analytical skill platform. Forecasting and simulated allocations do not involve executing trades, bets or financial contracts.

Are the probabilities live market data?

The current private-beta demo uses illustrative scenario data. Licensed market-data integrations and verified resolution feeds are planned, not represented as active.

Can I win cash prizes?

No real-money prize program is active. Any future prize program would require separate legal, jurisdiction, provider, finance and security approvals before activation.

How is forecasting skill evaluated?

FigSuite plans to emphasize calibration and proper scoring rules, including Brier scores, alongside sample-size and event-quality safeguards.
